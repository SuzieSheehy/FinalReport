\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
Throughout the implementation and testing of this rotating-covariance PCA-KF model structure, some optimisations have proven quite fruitful, such as the block diagonal numpy optimisation. Additionally, this rotating structure generated some negative results regarding the projection chaining approach, while also providing an insight into how static projections become stale over changing covariance structure. A useful future area of work would be in trying other projection-recovering approaches beyond the projection-chaining paradigm, sicne the latter appears to inherently suffer from an information loss problem.
